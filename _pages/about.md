  <p class="about-intro">
    Hi, I am <strong>Zhenyu Yi (伊振宇)</strong>. I am now a first-year
    M.S. student at <strong>Shanghai Jiao Tong University</strong>, advised by
    <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=YEYK0MoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN&oi=ao">Lichi Zhang</a> and
    <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=zh-CN&user=m6ZNDewAAAAJ">Qian Wang</a>. I also worked as a Research Intern at
    <a href="https://damo.alibaba.com/research-areas?language=en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">DAMO Academy, Alibaba Group</a>. Before that, I received my B.S. degree from
    <strong>Huazhong University of Science and Technology</strong> (2021-2025), where I collaborated with
    <a href="https://huster-hq.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Qiang Hu</a>.
  </p>

  <div class="research-scope">
    <p>
      My research focuses on <span style="font-weight:700; color:#1d4ed8;">computer vision</span> and
      <span style="font-weight:700; color:#4f46e5;">medical AI</span>, with particular interest in
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#7c3aed;">endoscopic image and video segmentation and diagnosis</span> and
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#2563eb;">brain imaging diagnosis</span>. I am also interested in
      <span style="font-weight:700; color:#be123c;">data-limited learning in medical scenarios</span>, including
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#be123c;">learning with noisy labels</span>,
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#dc2626;">semi-supervised learning</span>, and
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#ef4444;">weakly supervised learning</span>, to improve model robustness under realistic clinical annotation settings.
      In parallel, I explore <span style="font-weight:700; color:#4f46e5;">multimodal representation learning</span> through
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#6366f1;">vision-language alignment and pretraining</span>, and I further work on
      <span style="font-weight:700; color:#0f766e;">multimodal large language models</span>, especially
      <span style="font-weight:600; color:#0f766e;">efficient MLLMs</span> for clinically grounded reasoning and deployment.
    </p>
  </div>
